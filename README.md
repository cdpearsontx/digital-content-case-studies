# Digital Content Case Studies
This repository showcases selected projects and case studies involving digital accessibility, web content development, content management systems (CMS), and technical documentation.

The purpose of this portfolio is to demonstrate my approach to solving digital content challenges, with an emphasis on usability, accessibility, and practical solutions.

Each case study may include project background, challenges, tools and techniques used, solutions implemented, and outcomes.

## Areas of Focus
- Digital Accessibility: Remediation and testing of PDFs, PowerPoint presentations, web content, and other digital materials.
- Web Content and CMS Development: Content updates, component development, troubleshooting, and improvements to web publishing workflows.
- Content Strategy: Organizing, revising, and presenting information for clarity, consistency, and usability.
- Technical Documentation: Creating instructions, guides, and supporting documentation for technical processes.

## Case Studies
Case studies and supporting materials will be added as projects are completed and documented.

## About This Portfolio
Projects may include original work, representative examples, or adapted materials. Where appropriate, identifying or confidential information will be removed to protect organizational information.
